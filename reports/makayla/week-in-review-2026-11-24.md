Only the **~12 top comments per Instagram post** and Substack's public comments are visible logged out. TikTok comment text is behind a login. Sample: 103 labeled comments.

What this says:
- **Zero ad fatigue anywhere.** Affiliate posts read as "neutral" only because "Bags" or "link?" carries no sentiment. That's demand, not backlash.
- **The audience shops even when she isn't selling.** Posts with no links drew **11 unanswered product questions**: the sofa, rug, nursery dresser, baby lounger, window shade, changing-pad cover, postpartum belly wrap, matching set (×2), outfit, and the raspberry leaf tea. That's affiliate revenue going uncaptured.
- **Wellness posts draw the most questions** ("share your bed-making techniques", "what's the red drink?", "recipe plz"). Those are natural Substack hooks.
- **The only negative comment is a recipe complaint.** On Substack's White Chicken Chili, a reader wrote "only 1 can of beans and an entire jar of salsa?! Maybe typo?" Worth checking the recipe.
